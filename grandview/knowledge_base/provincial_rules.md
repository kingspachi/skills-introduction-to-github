# Provincial Rules — Reference Notes

> ⚠️ Confirm each province's current rules against the provincial authority and
> CRA. Numbers belong in `config/tax_rates_2025.yaml` (verified), not here.

## What varies by province
- **Personal income tax brackets & rates** (each province sets its own).
- **Provincial basic personal amount** and provincial credits.
- **Corporate small-business & general rates**, and the provincial SBD limit.
- **Sales tax model**: HST (ON, NB, NL, NS, PE), GST + PST (BC, SK, MB),
  GST only (AB + territories), GST + QST (QC, via Revenu Québec).
- **Payroll-related provincial levies** (e.g. health/employer levies in some
  provinces) — confirm applicability.
